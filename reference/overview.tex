\begin{abstract}
We audit an end-to-end effort to post-train Qwen3.5-2B for long-horizon play in Kaetram, an
open-source MMORPG exposed through 17 typed tools. Project notes report three relevant patterns.
First, a cross-vocabulary SFT lane fell below the reported base runs while aggregate tool use moved
toward the training-corpus marginal. Second, one natural-visitation reverse-KL OPD run changed tool
mix and interaction tempo without changing its 12/30 Core-3 score. Third, a later round that added
hand-selected persistent player-state starts was followed by a recovery-off 15/30 run and wall
passage across three clustered prompt variants; a subsequent weights-plus-recovery system run
reported 18/30. These are descriptive observations, not causal effects: there is one unmatched run
per cell, prompt variants are not independent seeds, and the June raw bundles, gameplay seeds,
checkpoint/configuration digests, and exact render contracts are absent. A historical probe also
reports positive teacher-over-student advantage for one malformed continuation, but it does not
score both canonical and malformed candidates in the same matched context and therefore does not
establish preference reversal. This report preserves the engineering history while explicitly
separating what was observed from what remains to be tested: matched fresh-student state-source
arms, training-level replication, a weights-by-recovery factorial, state-by-history controls,
held-out transfer, reset attestations, and immutable artifacts. The result is an audited case study
and confirmatory agenda, not an established method claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Can post-training instill genuinely long-horizon, tool-mediated competence in a $\sim$2B-parameter
model --- as opposed to surface style? This report traces a single project's complete answer-so-far
across three training paradigms, eleven rounds, and one environment, with every claim tied to
logged rollouts and code.

The task: autonomously progress three quest chains (``Core 3'', 10 stages total) in Kaetram, a 2D
MMORPG, via structured tool calls only. The metric: new quest stages per 6-hour run, summed over
three personality-variant agents (\emph{stages}/30). The arc:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{r1--r9 (SFT iteration).} Nine rounds of supervised fine-tuning of Qwen3.5-9B on
Claude-teacher trajectories established methodology (loss masking, prompt parity,
truncation gates, a $\sqrt{r}$ LoRA trap) but never beat the base model.
\item \textbf{r10 (exploratory negative).} All four reported base runs exceed all three reported
SFT runs. Aggregate decision-verb frequencies are consistent with marginal imitation, but raw
artifacts and matched-state probabilities are absent.
\item \textbf{r11a (scaffold scouting).} Reported harness and state-contract changes coincide with
large score movement across a small, duration-mismatched model-size sweep. The non-monotone sizes
motivate a harness hypothesis; they do not establish that capacity is irrelevant.
\item \textbf{r11b (this study's focus): three rounds of on-policy distillation}, scaffolded
Qwen3.5-4B (17/30) $\rightarrow$ base Qwen3.5-2B (12/30). Round one transferred the teacher's
\emph{behavioral distribution} while its reported score remains 12/30. A later state-augmented
round is followed by a recovery-off 15/30 run and wall passage across three clustered prompts.
Round three combines new weights and a recovery affordance and reports 18/30. Missing raw bundles,
exact parity, and gameplay seeds prevent causal attribution of the monotone descriptive sequence
$12 \rightarrow 12 \rightarrow 15 \rightarrow 18$.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Contributions.} (i) An evidence audit that separates descriptive behavior movement from
unsupported competence and mechanism claims. (ii) A prospective, falsifiable protocol for direct
persistent-player-state initialization, with matched reset/prefix baselines and a state-by-history
ablation; no method effect is currently claimed. (iii) A bounded tool-interface diagnostic:
historical notes report positive teacher-over-student advantage for one malformed continuation,
which motivates a paired copy-prior test but does not establish preference reversal. (iv) A
weights-by-recovery factorial and immutable-artifact contract designed to prevent the confounding
present in the historical 18/30 system result.

\section{System}

\subsection{Environment and benchmark}
Kaetram-Open is a tile-based MMORPG: a Node game server per agent (WebSocket), MongoDB
persistence, browser client. \textbf{Core 3} totals 10 stages: \emph{Foresting} (3 stages; two
10-log deliveries), \emph{Herbalist's Desperation} (3: accept $\rightarrow$ 3 bluelily
$\rightarrow$ 2 paprika + 2 tomato; herbs require Foraging level 5), \emph{Rick's Roll} (4:
talk $\rightarrow$ 5 cooked shrimp $\rightarrow$ a door puzzle $\rightarrow$ delivery). Two
mechanics make this hard for an LLM agent: \emph{skill gating} (resources refuse interaction
below the required level) and \emph{stochastic yield} --- each server tick a harvest succeeds with
low probability ($\approx$1/3 for an unleveled player, rising with skill level), and success
depletes the bush.
Long-horizon, partially random gathering --- not a scripted sequence. The metric, \textsc{Core-3},
is new stages reached: per agent-run the first-to-last observed stage delta (so resume-state replay
cannot inflate it), summed over the three agents, in $[0,30]$.

\subsection{Agent harness}
The agent runs an OODA loop (\texttt{play\_qwen.py}): an OpenAI-compatible client against a Modal
SGLang endpoint plus an MCP stdio client driving a Playwright-controlled browser. Decoding uses
Qwen3.5's thinking-mode sampling preset (temp 1.0, top-p 0.95, top-k 20, presence 1.5), but the
generation prompt is the template's \emph{non-thinking} default --- a closed-empty
\texttt{<think></think>} block, so reasoning surfaces as plain content (the dangling-\texttt{</think>}
dialect, \S\ref{sec:prefix}), a regime held identical across base, teacher, and student. Context budget 14{,}336 of
the 16{,}384 training length, measured with the same patched chat template the server renders;
overflow triggers a \emph{session rollover} (fresh conversation, persistent game state, a
programmatic --- never model-authored --- advisory note carrying quest stage and position).
The MCP server exposes 17 tools with three design principles: \emph{enforced affordances}
(attack auto-loots; navigate does BFS with snap-to-walkable; craft/buy auto-walk),
\emph{advisory state-contract} (\texttt{query\_quest} leads with canonical facts ---
accepted/stage/needed/have/remaining --- plus a recommendation the agent must verify;
\texttt{observe} enriches mobs with level/aggression and quests with item progress), and
\emph{payload compaction} (ASCII map dropped; $\sim$2$\times$ turns per session).
Three agents run in parallel (personality variants: grinder / completionist / explorer ---
priority modifiers only), each with its own game server, sandbox, and display;
\texttt{orchestrate.py} supervises; runs are launched from a wiped, freshly-seeded database.

\subsection{Training and serving infrastructure}
Each model size is a Modal SGLang app (student L4, teacher A100) exposing
\texttt{/v1/chat/completions} for rollouts and \texttt{/v1/score} for teacher-forced per-token
logprobs over a caller-supplied (context, full-text) pair --- raw pre-rendered strings, because
the Qwen3.5 repos ship different chat-template revisions but share one vocabulary, so an identical
string tokenizes identically on student and teacher. All components apply one chat-template patch
(the stock template silently strips \texttt{<think>} from intermediate turns; QwenLM/Qwen3
\#1831). Training runs on a Modal H100 with Unsloth + fresh LoRA ($r{=}64,\alpha{=}64$,
rsLoRA off); Qwen3.5's 3:1 Gated-DeltaNet hybrid requires pinned fast kernels
(\texttt{triton==3.3.1} + \texttt{fla 0.5.0}), which we adopted only after a three-way fp32
parity probe (fast-bf16 vs fallback-bf16 vs fp32 reference on real corpus tokens; the fast path
proved marginally \emph{closer} to fp32 than the fallback, grad cosines $\ge$0.997, $8.9\times$
speedup --- round-2 training: 50 minutes vs round-1's 8 hours).

\section{The SFT era (r1--r10): imitation collapse}

Rounds r1--r9 (Qwen3.5-9B, Claude-trajectory SFT) produced methodology, not wins: completion-only
loss masking (and the discovery that three rounds had silently trained full-token);
chat-template CoT preservation; byte-identical train/eval prompt parity; strict 16K truncation
gates; and the rsLoRA trap ($\alpha{=}r$ under rsLoRA is an $8\times$ effective-LR error that
diverged r7). r9 still lost to base.

\textbf{r10} is a motivating negative result whose raw artifact bundle is absent. Project notes
describe 8{,}510 training records from 15 Claude runs. They report base $7/30$ in \emph{all
four} eval runs (zero variance); r10-SFT scored $\{3,1,2\}$, mean $2.0/30$ --- a $3.5\times$
regression (Mann-Whitney exact, one-sided, $p{=}0.029$; Fisher's exact on Foresting completion
$p{=}0.016$, OR$=16$). The agent-level Fisher analysis is pseudo-replicated, and neither result is
independently regenerable from this clone. Aggregate tool mix is consistent with a hypothesis: the SFT policy's
decision-verb frequencies match the \emph{training-target} distribution within $\sim$1pp
(\texttt{interact\_npc} corpus 2.4\% $\leftrightarrow$ SFT 2.1\% vs base 10.8\%;
\texttt{query\_quest} 2.2 $\leftrightarrow$ 2.5 vs 11.2; \texttt{navigate} 27.7 $\leftrightarrow$
26.4 vs 5.5). Credit analysis showed why that marginal is poison: 52\% of source sessions advance
no quest; \texttt{interact\_npc} carries the highest near-term credit (36\% of uses precede an
advance within 5 turns) yet is 3.8\% of the corpus, while \texttt{attack} is 25.5\% of corpus
with $\approx$0\% credit. These aggregates motivate a corpus-prior hypothesis rather than a
matched-state mechanism result. Possible explanations include compounding
off-policy error (DAgger's $O(\epsilon T^2)$), capacity-gap marginal-fitting, CE diversity
collapse, and capability suppression.

\section{The scaffold reframe (r11a): harness scouting}

Before more training, we asked what a \emph{frozen} model could do with a better
model--environment contract. Over eight days the base-9B Core-3 best-run envelope rose through
$4$, $9$--$10$, $12$, $15$, and $19/30$ while harness/state-contract changes accumulated
(quest-list normalization, canonical \texttt{current\_step} facts, navigation hardening).
Durations and configurations differ, so the sequence does not identify each change's effect. The
reported full-run comparison is $7 \rightarrow 19$ (Table~\ref{tab:compare-progress}). It motivates
a possible knowing--doing gap at the boundary
between advisory and enforced behavior: every affordance that works is \emph{enforced} (auto-loot, BFS navigate); every
remaining blocker is \emph{advisory} (survival rules the model quotes and ignores).

The scaffold transfers across capacity. With identical scaffold and prompts:
27B $= 15/30$ ($n{=}1$, $\sim$4h), 9B $= 12$--$19/30$, 4B $= 12/30$ (3h) and $\mathbf{17/30}$
(5h), 2B $= 13/30$ (5h) and $12/30$ (6h). Foresting is solved at every size and Rick's Roll is
essentially untouched at every size (only the 9B's best run reaches Rick's stage 1); the
\emph{Herbalist wall} is where capacity shows --- the 2B stalls at stage 1 while 4B, 9B, and 27B
all clear it (the 27B also gained a tool-result confabulation failure mode). Above the 2B,
the observed scores are not monotone in capacity --- the 27B's 15 sits inside the 9B's
$12$--$19$ --- but durations and $n$ differ. The reported 4B 17 vs 2B 12 gap motivates the
distillation experiment: \textbf{teacher = scaffolded 4B, student =
base 2B} --- a real, same-family competence gap, an order of magnitude cheaper per round than the
9B lane. (Reference ceiling, on the pre-R11 Claude Code harness: in its best run $2/3$ of the
Claude agents complete \emph{all} of Core 3 including Rick's Roll --- $27/30$,
Table~\ref{tab:compare-progress} --- in 6h at 1{,}400--2{,}300 tool calls. This shows the game is
winnable, not a same-harness ceiling for the Qwen ladder.)

\textbf{Program-wide comparison.} Tables~\ref{tab:compare-progress} (quest progression \& scale)
and~\ref{tab:compare-tools} (tool-use signature) collect every run in this report, grouped by
harness era. They make three things visible at once: the \emph{harness lift} (the same base-9B
goes $7 \rightarrow 19$ from the pre-R11 to the R11 harness), the \emph{SFT regression} (r10-era

\section{On-policy distillation: method}

\subsection{Objective and loss}
Let $\pi_S$, $\pi_T$ be student and teacher, and $d^{\pi_S}$ the state distribution induced by
student rollouts (a ``state'' is the full serving prompt: system, bootstrap, dialogue history).
Batched reverse-KL \opd{} minimizes
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\textsc{opd}}
 \;=\; \mathbb{E}_{s \sim d^{\pi_S}}\;
 \mathbb{E}_{a \sim \pi_S(\cdot \mid s)}
 \Big[\, \log \pi_S(a \mid s) \;-\; \log \pi_T(a \mid s) \,\Big],
\label{eq:opd}
\end{equation}
the I-projection (mode-seeking) direction, estimated on the student's own emitted tokens with the
teacher as a per-token grader. We freeze a per-token advantage at data-build time --- the negated
student-minus-teacher logprob gap, taken against the \emph{behavior} (rollout-time) logprobs ---
and train with PPO-style clipped importance sampling ($\epsilon{=}0.3$, advantage clamp $\pm 3$,
plus a $1.5\times$ weight on each session's first third). The clip is load-bearing: unclipped
REINFORCE on a fixed batch's majority-negative advantages pays the optimizer to drive those
logprobs to $-\infty$ and degenerates the policy to an \texttt{observe} loop --- a collapse we
hit and the clip prevents. Because each round's init equals the rollout-generating policy
(fresh LoRA over the previous round's merged checkpoint), the importance ratio is $\approx 1$ at
step 0. The clipped objective discourages large local ratios but does not hard-bound probability
movement under shared-parameter updates; a step-0 calibration probe
verifies the serving-vs-training numeric gap (round 2: median $|{\Delta}\log p| = 0.0005$, clip
fraction $0.000$). LR $5\times10^{-5}$, one epoch, effective batch 32, H100; the LM head is
applied only at action positions (a full-vocab forward at $16\mathrm{K}\times 248\mathrm{K}$
would OOM).

\subsection{Visitation coupling and environment seeding}
\label{sec:visitation}
Equation~\ref{eq:opd}'s expectation over $d^{\pi_S}$ is the binding constraint this study makes
concrete: the teacher is only ever \emph{queried} at states the student visits. If the teacher's
competence edge lives at states of measure $\approx 0$ under $d^{\pi_S}$ --- e.g.\ standing at a
Blue Lily Bush with Foraging $\ge$5 --- no amount of dense grading transfers it. Round 1
demonstrates the failure; rounds 2--3 demonstrate the repair: change the \emph{state
distribution}, not the loss. Concretely we mix the student's natural visitation with rollouts of
$\pi_S$ launched from DB-seeded milestone states --- player rows written directly into the game's
MongoDB at e2e-verified mid-quest configurations (quest stage, skills, inventory, position),
$\sim$30\% of records by count. In the proposed matched experiment only the state source changes.
The historical rounds do not meet that condition: checkpoint initialization, collected policy,
record count, and data batch also differ. Resetting to hard or intermediate states to get learning signal there is a
well-trodden idea in RL --- reset along a demonstration~\cite{salchen}, Backplay's reverse
curriculum~\cite{backplay}, Go-Explore's restore-then-explore~\cite{goexplore}, and expert-state
reset mixtures for LLM agents~\cite{reset5050}; the LLM-side analogues operate in text-prefix or
policy space (branched expert anchors~\cite{bread}, behavior-policy blending~\cite{trb}). Our
narrow candidate contribution is a reverse-curriculum state reset
instantiated as a \emph{write of persistent, typed environment state} (a DB row), used for
on-policy \emph{distillation}. TCOD, Guided-OPD, ReOPD, SCoRe, Backplay, and TRB make a broad
state-curriculum claim unavailable; only a matched direct-player-state result could support the
narrower distinction.

\subsection{Data construction: the prefix property}
\label{sec:prefix}
Each action turn is rebuilt from rollout logs as the byte-exact serving prompt (system +
bootstrap + last 28 messages rendered through the patched template with
\texttt{add\_generation\_prompt}) plus the \textbf{verbatim logged emission} appended directly:
$\text{full} = \text{ctx} \,\|\, \text{emission}$. Re-rendering the completed turn through the
chat template is wrong in a subtle way we hit twice: the template extracts reasoning from any
\texttt{</think>} inside content and re-renders a real think block, diverging from the
closed-empty-think generation prompt the model was actually served --- 4\% of base-2B turns in
round 1, but $99\%$ of r1-policy turns (whose acquired dialect ends content with a
dangling \texttt{</think>}). Verbatim construction makes the prefix property hold by
construction; the round-2 build of 7{,}849 states had \emph{zero} alignment failures of any
kind. Both endpoints score the same raw string; behavior logprobs are therefore exactly the
rollout policy's serving distribution. Every 10th session is held out for the gate.

\subsection{Abstention masking and the gate}
\label{sec:gate}
Advantages are zeroed on token spans matching malformed parameter keys
(\texttt{<parameter=K=V>}) where the teacher's grading is probe-verified unreliable
(\S\ref{sec:copyprior}); 177 spans / 1{,}422 tokens (0.06\%) in round 2. (Round 3 grades these
same spans correctively instead of masking them, \S\ref{sec:round3}.) The post-train gate scores
the new student on the held-out set and reports the token-weighted reverse-KL to the teacher over
scorable action tokens (masked spans excluded). That number is \emph{directional only}: round 1
used a $\ge 30\%$ reduction as a PASS bar and it \emph{failed} ($+10.9\%$) while behavior moved
dramatically --- KAT~\cite{kat} shows per-token KL on teacher-agreement-trapped spans is a
misleading quality signal --- so rounds 2--3 gate hard on behavioral checks instead:
sampled-completion malformed-rate not worse than the prior round, no degeneration, and no
catastrophic reverse-KL blow-up. Round-2 gate $+14.5\%$, round-3 gate $+6.4\%$ (diminishing, as
expected once the student is near the teacher), both PASS.

\section{Round 1: style transfers, competence does not}

\textbf{Setup.} Corpus: the 2B's own 6h baseline rollouts (5{,}564 train / 574 held-out records)
re-scored by the 4B; the pre-train diagnostic put the largest student--teacher disagreement
exactly on the 2B's documented weak verbs (eat\_food $+0.298$ nats, respawn $+0.292$).
Training: 174 steps. Eval: 6h, 3 agents, protocol-identical to baseline (compact observes,
fresh DB, parity verified).

\textbf{Result: Core-3 $12/30$, identical to baseline} --- every agent 4/10 with the same split,
stuck at the same Herbalist stage-1 wall. Underneath, the student moved decisively onto the
teacher's distribution:

Execution improved where the signal pointed (navigate errors $5.3\%\!\rightarrow\!1.2\%$;

\subsection{The \texorpdfstring{KL$\times$behavior}{KL-times-behavior} cross-table}
\label{sec:crosstable}
The held-out gate moved only $+10.9\%$ (a formal FAIL against its 30\% bar) while behavior moved
dramatically --- and the per-verb \emph{signs} line up: every verb whose rKL dropped (attack
$-17\%$, stuck\_reset $-23\%$, warp, respawn, navigate) moved toward the teacher behaviorally,
and \texttt{query\_quest} --- the single verb whose rKL \emph{increased} ($+20\%$) --- is exactly
the verb that behaviorally overshot the teacher $2\times$: the student moved \emph{past} the
teacher and diverged from the other side. Small per-token movement compounds over autoregressive
rollouts; a token-KL gate on old-visitation states measures the wrong quantity.

\textbf{What the advantages reward: structure, not reasoning.} Reading the round-1 held-out
advantages directly (teacher minus student logprob over the emitted tokens), the positive
reverse-KL signal concentrates on tool-call \emph{structural} tokens --- the \texttt{</think>}
delimiter ($+0.212$, $79\%$ positive) and the \texttt{<function=}/\texttt{<parameter=} syntax
(sharply positive) --- while \emph{reasoning-prose} tokens carry the most \emph{negative} mean
advantage ($-0.333$; the tool-call region as a whole averages $\approx$0). The \texttt{</think>}
reward --- the dialect's entry hook --- explains the tag rate jumping $4\%\rightarrow99\%$; the
$\sim$4$\times$ growth in reasoning \emph{length} is instead a trajectory-level mode-seeking pull
toward the teacher's reason-then-act trajectories, \emph{not} a per-token reward. So \opd{} transfers
the \emph{shape} of reasoning, not its substance --- and the round-3 compression
(\S\ref{sec:round3}) is the same signal with that pull relaxed.

\subsection{The teacher-forcing copy prior}
\label{sec:copyprior}
Round 1 introduced a defect: 8.3\% of argument-requiring calls arrived with empty input,
dominated by one span family --- the model writes the boolean kwarg into the parameter
\emph{key}, \texttt{<parameter=accept\_quest\_offer=True>} (154 malformed vs 18 correct in the
r1 eval; 0 vs 182 in baseline), which the parser drops. Historical notes report teacher log
probability $-0.161$ for continuing that malformed candidate after malformed history, versus
$-0.253$ under the student. This is positive teacher-over-student distillation advantage for one
candidate, not evidence that the teacher prefers it over the canonical candidate. The reported
correct-form observation uses a different state, and the raw probe artifact is absent. A paired
clean/malformed-history by canonical/malformed-candidate experiment is therefore required. CCOPD,
SOD, KAT, and per-context OPD diagnostics also preempt a broad degraded-context novelty claim; the
remaining copy-prior framing is a hypothesis.

\section{Round 2: visitation-corrected distillation}

\textbf{Environment-state seeding.} The 4B teacher passes the Herbalist wall in its own
runs (2/3 agents finished the quest) --- the gap is real and teacher-demonstrated --- but
base-2B rollouts essentially never visit lily-bush-at-Foraging$\ge$5 states (20 gated attempts
in 6{,}183 calls). We therefore seeded the \emph{environment database}: player rows written
mid-quest (Herbalist stage 1 accepted, Foraging XP 450 $=$ level 5 on the game's XP curve,
starter kit, spawn at the e2e-verified walkable tile beside the lily cluster), then a 2.5h
collection run of the r1 policy from those states. The intended control point is $d^{\pi_S}$, but
the historical round differs in more than state source. In the seeded rollouts all
three agents passed the wall (the first 2B passages ever observed), producing both
student-authored success trajectories and dense teacher grades at decision states. This is a
reverse-curriculum state reset --- the RL lineage (reset-along-a-demo~\cite{salchen},
Backplay~\cite{backplay}, Go-Explore~\cite{goexplore}, expert-state resets~\cite{reset5050}) and
the LLM-side text-prefix/policy analogues (branched rollouts~\cite{bread}, behavior
blending~\cite{trb}); our instantiation, a write of \emph{persistent typed environment state} for
on-policy \emph{distillation} (not reward-RL), is what we have not seen published.

\textbf{Corpus and training.} 7{,}849 states (5{,}523 natural r1-eval rollouts + 2{,}326 seeded
wall states, interleaved) $\rightarrow$ 7{,}024 train / 825 held-out records, zero construction
failures; fresh-state per-verb rKL came in at $\approx$half of round-1 levels (eat\_food
$+0.298\!\rightarrow\!+0.149$; Herbalist frontier $+0.261\!\rightarrow\!+0.136$) --- round 1 had
genuinely moved the policy toward the teacher on its own visitation. 220 steps, 50 minutes.
Gate: \textbf{PASS} --- held-out rKL $0.4281 \rightarrow 0.3662$ ($+14.5\%$, \emph{every} verb
improved, including \texttt{query\_quest} reversing its round-1 sign), no degeneration, no
blow-up.

\textbf{Eval (6h, unseeded, parity verified): Core-3 $\mathbf{15/30}$ --- 5/10 for every agent.
All three agents passed the Herbalist stage-1 wall unseeded} (at 4.1h, 4.5h, 5.0h) --- a stage
neither base nor r1 ever reached: arm-level passage rates $0/3$, $0/3$,
$3/3$. Supporting movement: all accepts landed by 3.2h (prior arms' stragglers: 5.7h, 5.3h);
correct-form accepts $26$ vs $86$ malformed (r1: $18$ vs $154$); Foresting completion monotone
faster across rounds (0.4--0.6h vs 0.5--0.8 vs 0.7--1.8); the round-1 overshoots retreated
toward the teacher (query\_quest $18.0\!\rightarrow\!12.9\%$; verbosity
$1248\!\rightarrow\!1116$ chars/turn), with turns/session holding at the teacher's tempo
($6.1\!\rightarrow\!6.0$). Qualitatively, the
passage mechanism is visible in the logs: r2 agents rotate bushes after empty harvests, track the
Foraging-5 gate explicitly, and exploit gather's auto-walk; r1 at the same wall exhibited a
\emph{premature-turn-in attractor} (standing one tile from the bush holding 2/3 lilies, it walks
85 tiles to the quest NPC with an incomplete set) and gate-blind retries at Foraging 1--4; base
displaced into combat (a level-59 character with Foraging 4). At run end all three r2 agents were
mid-stage-2 (one paprika + tomatoes held; the completionist made the first-ever deliberate trip
toward the paprika cluster and nav-looped six tiles short).

\textbf{Costs.} (1) The malformed-call attractor \emph{mutated rather than died}: kwarg-in-key
declined (154$\rightarrow$86) but a new dominant form appeared --- Python-call syntax inside the
function tag, \texttt{<function=gather("Oak")>}: $0$ (base) $\rightarrow 79$ (r1) $\rightarrow
\mathbf{599}$ (r2) --- plus corrupted closing tags. Abstention masking removes reinforcement but
supplies no corrective pressure; the model's own malformed history compounds in-context. (2) The
\opd{} models carry a higher total failure rate than base (base 8.6\% / r1 16.9\% / r2 16.7\%),
driven by a validation-failure class ($\approx$0.1\% at base $\rightarrow$ $\approx$11\%) that the
malformed calls produce; the game-state error class, by contrast, improved across rounds
(8.5/5.5/5.9\%). (3) Deaths rose monotonically (142/172/188), though 78\% of r2 death positions
are on the quest NPC's tile inside a level-45--54 aggro zone: substantially the \emph{cost of
attempting the quest} (turn-ins survive death; agents name the danger and proceed).

\section{Round 3: counterfactual grading, full-ladder seeding, and a harness affordance}
\label{sec:round3}

Round 3 targeted the two open wounds --- the mutated malformed-call attractor and the stalled
Herbalist stage 2 --- with two weights-side moves and, ultimately, a harness one.
\textbf{Counterfactual-canonicalized grading:} at parser-flagged records the teacher scores the
student's emission under a \emph{canonicalized} context $s'$ --- the system prompt's Python-style
tool-doc literals rewritten to canonical wire syntax --- while the student/behavior side keeps the
real context $s$. Scoring the teacher under $s'$ turns its clean-convention preference into a
corrective negative advantage on the malformed tokens, rather than round-1's $+0.09$ copy-prior
endorsement. This is a designed reward, not an unbiased reverse-KL estimate (student and teacher
are scored under different contexts); we adopt it deliberately at exactly the spans where the
visitation context poisons the grade. A pre-build \emph{flip probe} set the scope empirically:
canonicalizing the malformed \emph{history} calls did nothing (a measured $0\%$ flip), but
rewriting the system prompt's Python-style tool-doc literals in the teacher's grading copy flipped
$86\%$ of flagged states at $-1.21$ nats --- so $s'$ rewrites the doc literals only.
\textbf{Full-ladder seeding:} two seeded collections placed the 2B at every unsolved stage
(Herbalist stage 2; Rick's fishing/cook/turn-in/door), all graded by the plain 4B. Build:
8{,}856 train / 1{,}040 held-out, 4 score failures; 365 counterfactually-graded records (zero
masked --- corrective grading replaced abstention). Gate PASS ($+6.4\%$ rKL, every verb).

\textbf{The decisive negative result.} The first eval was stopped at 35 minutes:
counterfactual grading did \emph{not} suppress the malformed \emph{emission} --- it regressed it,
paralyzing two of three agents (57--76\% of their actions were malformed calls the server drops,
which fall to text, never execute, and the model re-emits in a spam loop).
Historical notes say the grading-context intervention did not suppress student emissions in this
stopped run. A \emph{harness affordance} then supplied a runtime workaround: when the server
drops a malformed call, the harness recovers the executable call (99.5\% coverage), rewrites
history to a clean canonical turn (severing the in-context copy prior at its source), executes
it, and returns a loud format-correction note.

\textbf{Reported result: $\mathbf{18/30}$ for the weights-plus-recovery system, $6/10$ per prompt
variant.} Every agent now completes two Core-3 chains unseeded --- Foresting \emph{and}
Herbalist --- where base completes one (Foresting, then stalls at the Herbalist wall). All three
completed Herbalist 3/3 unseeded (round 2 had zero), breaking the stage-2 wall via three
concrete differences from r2's stuck behavior: navigating to the \emph{actual} paprika bush
(deep in the hostile zone) rather than the prompt's stale coordinate; recovering from a one-tile
navigation stall by re-targeting an adjacent tile (where r2 looped identically $\sim$16 times);
and a tighter observe--act loop (half-length reasoning re-observes often enough to notice a
frozen position) --- with gathered items surviving death. The harness recovery worked and
historical notes describe as repeated-marker-free: 405 rewritten sessions are reported to contain
one format note and no later marker. The source logs and raw pre-rewrite emissions are not
packaged, so this cannot establish model correction or relapse prevention. The same notes report
validation/format errors halving ($10.7\% \rightarrow 5.1\%$); malformed
emissions numbered 335 versus r2's reported 685. Reasoning
compressed sharply against round 2: non-empty median 243 chars vs r2's 558, $\sim$30\% near-empty
turns (r2: $0\%$), revision-churn down ($59\% \rightarrow 30\%$), and the
dangling-\texttt{</think>} dialect abandoned ($99\% \rightarrow 12\%$), with grounding preserved.

\textbf{The historical runs do not identify a causal decomposition.} The notes report base
$12/30$, round two at $15/30$ without recovery, round-two weights with recovery at $17/30$, and
round-three weights with recovery at $18/30$. Each cell contains one unmatched run; raw bundles,
exact checkpoint and render parity, gameplay seeds, and immutable manifests are absent. Score
subtraction therefore cannot identify weights, recovery, or their interaction. The recovery-off
round-two wall passage across three clustered prompts is the strongest historical observation,
but it is neither three independent replications nor a pure-weights estimate. The reported
throughput contrast ($\sim$40\% more turns per hour) is likewise descriptive until reproduced
under matched runs.

\textbf{Rick's Roll stayed $0/4$ --- a three-link execution failure, not missing intent.} The
agents understood and attempted the full chain (52 navigations to Rick, 5 NPC-interaction
attempts, 12 door attempts, all quoting the walkthrough). The chain dies at link one: the fishing
pole is never re-equipped (Foresting's logs displaced it), so every shrimp gather returns nothing
and cook/door/Rick never become reachable; \texttt{craft\_item} was called \emph{zero} times in
all post-Herbalist sessions --- exactly the cook-incompetent-teacher prediction (the 4B never
cooks, so its seeded-state grades there were weak). Historical notes therefore motivate three
separate hypotheses: visitation can expose teacher competence, recovery can address an interface
failure, and an incompetent teacher cannot supply useful local supervision. The 18/30 arm is a
weights-plus-recovery configuration, not a test that identifies any of those effects.

\section{What is and is not valid}

\textbf{Supported observations.} (a) The r10 SFT arm fell below base in every one of seven
exploratory runs (base $[7,7,7,7]$, SFT $[3,1,2]$; unadjusted exact tests
$p{=}0.029$ / $0.016$). The aggregate tool-frequency match is a mechanism hypothesis, not a
matched-state causal result. (b) In a small, duration-mismatched capacity sweep, larger models did
not monotonically improve Core-3 score; this is scouting evidence, not a scaling conclusion.
(c) Round-1 style-without-competence transfer (three
independent measurement families agree: tool mix, tempo, per-verb KL signs). (d) The copy-prior
mechanism hypothesis (an unpaired historical probe; alternatives remain open). (e) The round-2
lift as a descriptive observation across clustered prompts, not a causal estimate. (f) The round-3 results: the Herbalist stage-2 break and its three-part
mechanism (correct coordinate, stall-recovery, tight loop --- all read directly from the
transition sessions); the reported harness-recovery marker count ($405$ rewritten sessions,
with source logs not packaged); the grader-vs-generation gap (the flip
probe verified $-1.21$ nats of endorsement suppression, yet the stopped run shows the emission
\emph{regressed} --- a reported falsifying observation); and the reported cook null
($\texttt{craft\_item}=0$, pole-equipped $=0$ across all agents).

\textbf{Faults and caveats, enumerated.}
\emph{(1) The $18/30$ arm is not pure-weights.} It ran with the harness tool-recovery affordance
on; base/r1/r2 did not. The unmatched round-2-weights-plus-recovery run reports $17/30$, but raw
bundles, exact configuration parity, and gameplay seeds are absent. It cannot decompose the
round-two-to-round-three difference. Round two's $15/30$ wall passage is recovery-off and the
least-confounded historical observation, not a pure-weights effect. \emph{(2) $n{=}1$ per arm}, agents are prompt
variants not independent seeds; direction-consistency is suggestive, not significant. Stage
counts are coarse ($\pm 2$--$3/30$ historical band); error-rate and milestone analyses carry the
most descriptive detail but no population inference. The r10 negative is the only historical
comparison with multiple reported runs, but its raw bundle is absent; the beyond-base claim --- even
at $3/3$ vs $0/3$ on the wall --- rests on prompt-variant arms that share a base policy and so
are a partial (not independent) replicate, which is why we lean on the categorical wall passage
rather than the stage delta. The obvious next validation is seed replication of the round-two
passage, which we have not run. \emph{(3) The malformed-emission counter is a lower bound.} The harness
rewrites assistant content to canonical before logging, so the counter catches only malformed text
that survived recovery (historical notes report $335$ logged vs $405$ recovery markers); the true
round-3 malformation count sits between the two. \emph{(4) The mixed-context advantage is a
designed reward, not an unbiased KL estimate}: the teacher is scored under the canonicalized
context while the student is scored under the real one. CCOPD~\cite{ccopd} uses the same move
without a bias analysis, so we inherit that gap. \emph{(5) Residual analyzer caveats}:
quest-transition verb attribution reports the in-flight tool not the cause; \texttt{gather} item
counts race the server and under-report successes; the eat\_food error class conflates
full-HP/slot-shift with validation empties. \emph{(6) Costs are billing-verified} (\texttt{modal
billing report}, as for r10's \$360): r1--r3 totaled \$160 incl. all debugging
($\sim$44\% of one r10 SFT cycle), but the per-round split is the opposite of intuitive ---
round 1 $\approx$\$111 (dominated by a 3$\times$ broken-kernel $\sim$8h H100 retrain at
$\sim$168\,s/step), round 2 $\approx$\$33, round 3 $\approx$\$16. Once the fast GDN kernels were
fixed (round 2) the marginal build+train of a round is $\sim$\$5--10; eval serving on L4
($\sim$\$6--23/round) then dominates. No paid-API cost enters the loop --- student rollouts and
the 4B teacher are both self-hosted Qwen on Modal. \emph{(7) The decode regime is non-thinking, by
inheritance not design.} Every model ran the Qwen3.5 template's default closed-empty
\texttt{<think></think>} prompt (the thinking-mode \emph{sampling} preset sits on top of it), so
reasoning surfaces as plain content; we did not deliberately choose thinking vs.\ non-thinking. It is
held identical across base, teacher serve, teacher grading, and every student eval, so it is an
internal-consistency property, not a confound on any comparison --- whether thinking-on would change
the results is untested.

\textbf{Not claimed.} No general statement about 2B capacity ceilings (Rick's failure is
multi-causal --- a displaced tool, a budget limit, and a teacher null --- not a clean capacity
result). No claim that grading-context fixes cure generation defects (the stopped run falsifies
it). No claim that masking cures teacher-shared defects (the attractor mutated $154 \rightarrow
86$ on one form while a new form went $0 \rightarrow 599$). No cross-environment generality;
findings are conditional on a deliberately strong scaffold whose transfer is untested.

\section{Related work}
\textbf{Game-agent systems.} Orak uses MCP as a plug-and-play interface across twelve games and
includes a gameplay fine-tuning dataset~\cite{orak}; MCP and structured game actions are therefore
infrastructure here, not a novelty claim. BALROG evaluates long-horizon language and
vision-language agents across games and documents a knowing--doing gap~\cite{balrog}, while
lmgame-Bench studies modular gaming harnesses and transfer from single-game training to unseen
games and external planning tasks~\cite{lmgame}. Unlike these benchmark-centered systems, our
question is how a small policy's persistent environment-state visitation controls what dense
teacher grading can transfer.

\textbf{On-policy distillation.} Reverse-KL on-policy distillation for LLMs was established by
GKD~\cite{gkd} and popularized as a post-training recipe by Thinking Machines~\cite{tml}; the 2026 wave maps its failure modes: trust-region restriction of unreliable
teacher tokens~\cite{tropd}, token-subset selection~\cite{tip,teachability}, structural-token
masking~\cite{simplefixes,rocktokens}, verifier-gated sign-consistency~\cite{sgopd},
trajectory-filter-then-soft-reweight~\cite{fire}, and the KL agreement trap, where low per-token
KL on degraded prefixes is the failure signature~\cite{kat}; DistIL proves reverse-KL-family
objectives can raise probability on worse actions~\cite{distil}. Our historical copy-prior probe
does not yet establish a wrong-signed preference; that requires the paired candidate study. The
visitation-coupling lens is formalized in States-not-Tokens~\cite{snt},
and our round-1 outcome --- style-level convergence without capability gain --- is its documented
failure mode when the teacher's edge lies outside the student's visited states~\cite{rethinking};
state-distribution control by resetting to hard/intermediate states is well-trodden in RL
(reset-along-a-demo~\cite{salchen}, Backplay~\cite{backplay}, Go-Explore~\cite{goexplore}) and
appears LLM-side as branched rollouts from expert anchors~\cite{bread}, expert-state reset
mixtures~\cite{reset5050}, resume-from-verified-prefix~\cite{score}, and behavior-policy
blending~\cite{trb}. Our prospective distinction is persistent typed player state independent of a
successful prefix. Multi-turn agentic \opd{} at small scale documents per-turn KL
escalation and curricula~\cite{tcod}; iterated rounds are the DAgger loop~\cite{dagger,survey}.
The harness-scouting hypothesis aligns with the 2026 system-scaling literature (interface
adaptation over weights)~\cite{lifeharness,systemscaling}. The r10 observation connects to imitation
collapse / capability-suppression results in SFT~\cite{gem}.

\textbf{Grading-context engineering (round 3).} Scoring student tokens under a teacher context
that differs from the generative context is an emerging family: CCOPD grades under a
\emph{canonical} clean context to fight self-anchored drift~\cite{ccopd}; OPCD conditions the
teacher on internalized context~\cite{opcd}; MixSD switches grading context per token~\cite{mixsd};
CoD combines original and counterfactually-perturbed teacher contexts off-policy~\cite{cod}. Our
canonicalization of the teacher's grading context (the system-prompt tool-doc literals) is a
historical intervention whose effect on generation is not established. CCOPD preempts broad
clean-context novelty; a matched tool-candidate probe is required for any narrower claim.
\textbf{Privileged-information distillation} is a relevant route for Rick's Roll, where
the same-family teacher is incompetent: a PI-conditioned teacher (verified or frontier-model
trajectories in its context) scores the student's rollouts token-by-token~\cite{pidistill,opsd},
with evidence-masked updates~\cite{edgeopd}; this enters as prompt text and so sidesteps the
cross-vocabulary problem that sank our SFT line, and logit-free chunk-similarity distillation
extends it to black-box teachers~\cite{omniopd}. On the negative-gradient question, AOPD finds bare
unlikelihood reinforcement ineffective and prefers corrective distribution-matching~\cite{aopd}
(supporting our corrective-grade-over-mask choice), while AntiSD demonstrates a sign-reversed
self-distillation term under an entropy gate~\cite{antisd}. The copy-prior mechanism's substrate
is in-context copy bias and induction-head dynamics~\cite{copybias,repetitioncurse}.

\section{Discussion and future work}
The historical sequence sharpens the open agenda but does not confirm a mechanism. Full-ladder
seeding, new weights, and recovery coincide in the later rounds; their contributions remain
unidentified. Rick's $0/4$ is likewise multi-causal: a displaced fishing tool gates link one,
the traverse exceeds a session budget, and cooking is never reached. The next useful work is
therefore controlled rather than another narrative round: (i) train fresh matched students across
natural OPD, direct player-state resets, generic resets, TCOD-style prefixes, and faithful
Guided-OPD; (ii) cross weights with recovery while preserving raw emissions; (iii) test privileged
context at matched cost and on a held-out quest; and (iv) score canonical and malformed candidates
under paired histories. The grading-side probe is a hypothesis about generation, not a guarantee.

\section{Conclusion}
The historical record motivates three hypotheses rather than establishing three effects. First,
the reported natural-OPD round changes tool use and tempo while its score remains 12/30. Second,
a later state-augmented, recovery-off round is followed by 15/30 and wall passage across three
clustered prompts. Third, a weights-plus-recovery run reports 18/30, while Rick's Roll remains
$0/4$. One unmatched run per cell, missing June raw bundles, absent gameplay seeds, and incomplete
configuration provenance prevent causal attribution or population inference. The durable result
of this report is therefore an audited failure analysis and a matched confirmatory design. Fresh
training replicates, reset attestations, a weights-by-recovery factorial, prefix/reset baselines,
held-out quests, and paired copy-prior artifacts must decide whether the proposed mechanisms
survive.
